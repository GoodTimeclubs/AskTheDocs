# Travel Expense Policy

**Valid from:** 1 September 2025
**Owner:** Finance Department, contact: Jonas Okafor (CFO)

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Nordlicht Robotics GmbH travelling on business, including trips between the Kassel, Leipzig and Graz sites.

## 2. Transport

- Train travel is the default for trips under **600 km**. Second class is standard; first class is allowed for trips over 4 hours.
- Flights require prior approval from the department head.
- Private car use is reimbursed at **€0.30 per km**.

## 3. Accommodation

| City | Max. rate per night |
|------|---------------------|
| Kassel | €110 |
| Leipzig | €120 |
| Graz | €130 |
| Stockholm | €190 |
| Other | €140 |

## 4. Meals (per diem)

- Full day (24 h): €28
- Partial day (over 8 h): €14
- If breakfast is included in the hotel, €5.60 is deducted.

## 5. Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted via the Polaris portal within **30 days** after the trip. Receipts must be uploaded as PDF or photo. Late submissions may be rejected.
